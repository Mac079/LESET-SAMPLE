✦ Copywriter's Notes: What Changed & Why

The Hero

The original hero was purely visual. Beautiful image, "Shop Now," nothing else. A first-time visitor had no idea what LESET stood for before being asked to buy. The rewrite names the woman's actual life (coffee into lunch, office into evening) so she feels understood before she feels sold to. That order matters.

The Brand Statement

The origin story lives on the About page where most visitors never reach it. This version brings it forward to the homepage, framed around Lili personally. "The brand that invented a category" is compelling. "That's what Lili set out to make" is human. Founder energy is the most underused conversion asset on the LESET site right now.

The Email Capture

"Sign up and get 10% off" is the same thing every other brand says. The rewrite removes the discount entirely and replaces it with access and scarcity. LESET's drops sell out. That is a stronger offer than 10% off. It respects the subscriber's intelligence and makes signing up feel like an advantage, not a transaction.
